SO/ FACET Game Privacy Policy

Version 2.2 | Effective from the date the Game goes live | Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you visit sofacet.com before SO/ FACET launches: when you play the SO/ FACET pinball game (the Game), save a score, appear on the leaderboard, earn SO/ COINS, join THE SO/ COLLECTIVE or contact us. When SO/ FACET launches, the SO/ FACET store will have its own privacy policy, which we will show you before you place an order. Words such as SO/ COINS, Verified High Score, Display Name and Launch have the meaning given in our Game Terms and Conditions.

1 Who we are

1.1 Caelura Corp. Private Limited, CIN U32119MP2025PTC079485, trading as SO/ FACET (we, us or our), decides how and why your personal information is used for the Game and this website. Registered office: Unit 3, Second Floor, 39 Mangal Nagar, Rajiv Gandhi Square, AB Road, Indore 452001, Madhya Pradesh, India.

1.2 Privacy contact: privacy@sofacet.com.

1.3 Grievance Officer: Abhishek Naulakha, Director, grievance@sofacet.com, at the registered office address above.

1.4 You do not need to agree to marketing to contact us.

2 Summary

  • You can play as a guest without giving us your name, email or mobile number.
  • To save a score and earn SO/ COINS, you must be 18 or older and give us your full name, mobile number and email address.
  • Your Display Name (your first name and the initial of your last name), Verified High Score and rank are shown on the public leaderboard. Your full name and contact details are never shown.
  • Our website uses essential cookies and similar technologies to run the site. Only if you agree, it may also use technologies from Meta, Google, Microsoft and PostHog to understand how the site is used and to measure and improve our advertising. Some of these record how you use the website, with anything you type hidden. They are not in use at present. Section 9 explains your choices.
  • Our website is hosted in the United States. Section 11 explains where your information is stored.
  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your gameplay to personalise content or to train artificial intelligence models.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, and you can unsubscribe from marketing at any time.

3 Guests and people under 18

3.1 You can play the Game as a guest without giving us your name, email or mobile number. Your game settings, such as sound, are stored in your browser until you or the website clear them. Your game is not sent to us unless you save your score. Even so, some information is collected automatically when you visit, such as your IP address, device and browser details and how you use the website (see sections 5 and 9).

3.2 People under 18 may play as guests only. Saving a score, joining the leaderboard, earning SO/ COINS, joining THE SO/ COLLECTIVE and any Recognition are for people aged 18 or over. We ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older before you can save a score or join our list.

3.3 We do not knowingly collect contact details from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has given us their details, we will delete them, together with any saved score and SO/ COINS, except where the law requires us to keep them. A parent or guardian who believes this has happened can contact privacy@sofacet.com. We will not ask for more information than is reasonably needed to deal with the request.

3.4 We do not intend our advertising for people under 18 and we set our advertising campaigns to reach adults.

4 Information you give us

4.1 When you save a score: your full name; your Display Name, which is taken from your full name; your mobile number; your email address; the score you ask us to save; your confirmation that you are 18 or older; your acceptance of our Game Terms and this policy (with the date, time, IP address and version); your marketing choice; and the result of the OTP check. We do not verify your email address separately.

4.2 When you join THE SO/ COLLECTIVE without playing: your full name, mobile number and email address, your confirmation that you are 18 or older, your acceptance of our Game Terms and this policy (with the date, time, IP address and version) and your agreement to receive updates from us.

4.3 When you contact us: your contact details, your message, any relevant score or profile reference, and anything else you choose to send. Please do not send passwords, OTPs, bank details or identity documents unless we ask for them through a secure channel.

4.4 If we offer Recognition: if we ever offer Recognition to top scorers, we will tell you at that time what information we need (for example, to confirm your age or to arrange an invitation). We will ask for identity documents only from people who are being considered for Recognition, and only where necessary.

4.5 We do not ask for your date of birth, city, photograph, postal address or payment details to save a score.

5 Information collected automatically

5.1 Gameplay information: game session identifiers, scores, Verified High Scores, the server time at which scores are recorded, leaderboard rank, SO/ COINS earned, used, restored or expired, and the results of our checks for fair play. When you submit a score, we check it against your signed-in session and a valid game session. The game data sent for that check is not stored.

5.2 Technical information: IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city level), browser and device type, operating system, referring page, the link you arrived from (for example, a creator's link), errors, and server logs recording your IP address, browser, the time and the request made. We do not collect your precise location, contacts or other device permissions, and we do not use device fingerprinting.

5.3 How you use the website: the pages and sections you view and scroll through, clicks, starting, pausing and ending a game, lives played, new high scores, scores saved, profiles created and signing in. Our session-recording tools also record mouse movements, taps, clicks and scrolling so that we can see how the website is used. Anything you type into a form is hidden from these recordings.

5.4 Cookies and similar technologies: see section 9 and the technology schedule in section 17.

5.5 Information from service providers: the result of the OTP check from our messaging provider, and reports from Meta, Google, Microsoft and PostHog about how our website and advertising perform. We do not buy personal information from data brokers or use social login.

7 Public leaderboard

7.1 When you save a score, your Display Name, Verified High Score and rank appear on the public leaderboard, which anyone visiting the website can see. Saving a score includes appearing on the leaderboard. Our Game Terms, which you accept when you save a score, explain this.

7.2 Your Display Name is your first name and the initial of your last name (for example, "Riya K."), unless you change it in My SO/ COINS. We never show your full name, email or mobile number.

7.3 Leaderboard entries stay visible while the leaderboard is active. Other people may see or copy them; we cannot remove copies made outside our website.

7.4 If we ever announce Recognition, we will tell you before it starts what information about recognised participants we will publish. We will ask for your separate permission before using your photograph, interview or testimonial.

8 Messages we send

8.1 Service messages. We send messages that are necessary for the Game, such as OTPs, score confirmations, your SO/ COINS balance and expiry, notice of the Launch and information needed to use your SO/ COINS, changes to our terms, and security notices. We send them by WhatsApp, SMS or email. You cannot unsubscribe from these while you have a saved score, because they relate to your SO/ COINS.

8.2 Marketing messages. We send marketing (for example, news about the Launch and our collections) by email, SMS and WhatsApp only if you agree to receive it. Saying no, or unsubscribing later, does not affect your score or SO/ COINS. You can unsubscribe from any channel using the link or instructions in any marketing message, or by emailing privacy@sofacet.com. We will act on it promptly, but a message already scheduled for that day may still reach you. We keep a minimal record of your choice so that we respect it.

8.3 Our earlier waitlist. If you joined our waitlist before the Game launched, we use your details only for the purpose explained when you joined. Joining the waitlist did not create a game profile or leaderboard entry.

8.4 How OTPs are sent. We send OTPs by WhatsApp. Once our SMS sender ID and templates are registered on the Distributed Ledger Technology platform, as Indian telecom regulations require, we may also send them by SMS. An OTP is valid for 5 minutes, and you can try it 3 times.

9 Cookies, pixels and similar technologies

9.1 Cookies are small files stored in your browser. Pixels and tags are small pieces of code that send information to us or to a service provider when you visit a page. Session-recording tools capture how you move through a page. We use these technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential: to make the website and Game work, remember your game settings, keep you signed in, protect the website against attacks and fraud, and remember your cookie choices. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics, our own analytics service, Microsoft Clarity and PostHog help us understand how the website is used, which links bring visitors and where things go wrong. Microsoft Clarity and PostHog also make recordings of how visitors use the website, with anything typed into a form hidden. Microsoft may also use information collected through Clarity for its own purposes, including improving its products and services and advertising, as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Advertising and measurement: the Meta Pixel and Google tags help us measure how our ads perform, show our ads to people who have visited our website, and find people who may be interested in SO/ FACET. Meta and Google receive information such as the pages you visit, actions you take on our site (for example, starting a game or saving a score), your IP address and browser details, and their cookie identifiers. They do not receive your name, email or mobile number from us. Meta and Google also use this information under their own privacy policies.

9.2 Your choices. When you first visit our website, our cookie notice asks for your choice. Analytics and advertising technologies are used only if you choose "Accept all" or switch them on in Cookie settings. If you choose "Reject optional", we use essential technologies only. You can change your choice at any time through Cookie settings, which is available on every page. Your choice does not affect the Game or your SO/ COINS.

9.3 At present, the analytics and advertising technologies that need your agreement are not in use. If we start using them, they will be used only with your agreement through our cookie notice.

9.4 You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Clearing your browser storage may delete an unsaved score or your game settings. Browser settings may not stop all server-side data collection.

9.5 Section 17 lists the technologies we use, who provides them, why we use them and how long they last.

10.1 We share personal information only as needed for the purposes in section 6, with:

  • Service providers who act for us, such as website hosting, database and storage, security and content delivery, WhatsApp, SMS and email delivery, analytics and session recording, and the developers who build and maintain the Game. Section 18 lists them.
  • Meta and Google, through the advertising technologies described in section 9.
  • Authorities and advisers, where the law requires it, or where needed to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business or the Game is transferred, under confidentiality obligations and on the condition that they use the information in line with this policy.

10.2 Service providers processing information solely on our behalf may use your information only on our instructions, must keep it confidential and secure, and must delete or return it when their work ends.

10.3 We do not sell your personal information. We do not upload your email address or mobile number to advertising platforms. If we ever wish to, we will ask for your consent first.

Our service providers listed in section 18 may store or access information in the United States and other countries, and our team and developers may access it from India and elsewhere. Where information is transferred outside India, we take the steps required by Indian law and by the law that applies to the information.

11.2 Server logs are kept in India for 180 days, as Indian cyber-security rules require.

13 Security

13.1 We use reasonable security measures appropriate to the information we hold. All traffic to the website is encrypted, personal information is encrypted when stored, access to production systems is restricted to the SO/ FACET team and our developers and requires multi-factor authentication, and scores are validated on our servers. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and keep our measures under review.

13.2 If a security incident affects your personal information, we will take reasonable steps to contain it and will notify you and the relevant authorities where the law requires.

13.3 Please report any suspected misuse to security@sofacet.com. Never share your OTP with anyone.

14 Your rights and choices

14.1 You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, such as for marketing; and
  • deal with a complaint about how we use your information.

14.2 Deleting your information removes your SO/ COINS. We need your details to keep your saved score and SO/ COINS. If you ask us to delete them, your saved score, Display Name and SO/ COINS will be permanently removed. We will tell you this and ask you to confirm before we act. We may keep limited records where the law requires it.

14.3 Additional rights, such as the right to nominate another person to exercise your rights, will apply when the relevant provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 come into force. If you are in another country, you may have further rights under your local law.

14.4 To make a request, email privacy@sofacet.com from the email address you gave us, or include the mobile number linked to your profile. We may need to verify your identity before acting. We will acknowledge your request within 48 hours and respond within one month, or any shorter period required by law. We aim to respond within 15 days.

14.5 If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact our Grievance Officer (section 1.3). You may also complain to any authority that has jurisdiction under applicable law, including the Data Protection Board of India once the relevant provisions are in force.

15 Your SO/ FACET account and the Launch

15.1 The profile you create when you save a score is your SO/ FACET account. It is the same account across SO/ FACET's websites and services, and you sign in with your mobile number. From Launch, you use it at checkout to apply your SO/ COINS. We will not turn a marketing choice you made for the Game into a broader permission.

15.2 Before we collect payment or delivery details, we will show you the SO/ FACET store's privacy policy.

16 Changes to this policy

16.1 We may update this policy at any time, for example when our practices or the law change. We will publish the updated policy on this page with a new date.

16.2 If we make a material change, we will tell registered participants in advance by email, SMS, WhatsApp or a notice on the website, and make the updated policy available before you next save a score or use SO/ COINS. Where the law requires your consent to a change, we will ask for it and will not treat your silence or continued use as consent.

16.3 This policy is available in English.
