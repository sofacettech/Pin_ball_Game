The original selected music and impact MP3s are preserved without re-encoding. The rolling extract is 44.1 kHz, 16-bit mono PCM. Its playback loop starts at 0.12 seconds after the first pass to meet the crossfaded tail. Sound attempts to start automatically; when browser autoplay is blocked, the first click/tap or keyboard interaction enables it. The bottom-left AUDIO ON/OFF button controls mute. Music position carries across About and Privacy navigation when browser playback policy permits. Hidden tabs pause audio. At queen contact, music and rolling fade immediately; the landing audio context suspends after 0.50 seconds so the impact is not cut short. No game-end victory cue is included.
