Schedule A: SO/ COINS

A1 Dates

Game Launch: 1 November 2026. Launch: expected in December 2026, with the date announced on the website; this is an estimate, not a promise. All times are India Standard Time (UTC+05:30).

A2 How SO/ COINS are earned

Your SO/ COINS equal your Verified High Score. For example, a Verified High Score of 32,000 earns 32,000 SO/ COINS. If you later achieve a higher Verified High Score, you earn SO/ COINS only for the difference.

A3 What SO/ COINS are worth and the order cap

10 SO/ COINS are equal to ₹1 of discount. On each order, the discount is the lower of:

  • (a) the value of your Available SO/ COINS (₹1 for every 10 SO/ COINS); and
  • (b) 10% of the combined diamond value and making charges of the eligible items in the order, as shown on our website.

You apply the discount by switching on SO/ COINS at checkout. The discount is applied to the diamond value and making charges only, in the same proportion. It does not apply to the metal value, GST, shipping or any other charge. GST is charged on the price after the discount. The discount is calculated in whole rupees, and SO/ COINS are used in multiples of 10. The 10% figure is a maximum, not a guaranteed discount on every order.

A4 Worked examples

Example 1. You have 32,000 SO/ COINS (worth ₹3,200). You order a piece with a diamond value of ₹20,000, making charges of ₹5,000 and metal value of ₹15,000, plus GST. The cap is 10% of ₹25,000 (diamond value plus making charges), which is ₹2,500. You use 25,000 SO/ COINS for a ₹2,500 discount: ₹2,000 off the diamond value and ₹500 off the making charges. GST is charged on the reduced price. 7,000 SO/ COINS (worth ₹700) remain for a later order.

Example 2. You have 8,000 SO/ COINS (worth ₹800) and place the same order. The cap is ₹2,500, so you use all 8,000 SO/ COINS for an ₹800 discount.

Example 3. Your Verified High Score is 32,000 and you use 25,000 SO/ COINS, leaving 7,000. You later achieve a Verified High Score of 40,000. You earn 8,000 more SO/ COINS (the improvement), so you now have 15,000 Available SO/ COINS. Scoring 32,000 again would add nothing.

A5 Expiry

SO/ COINS earned during the Pre-launch Period expire at 11:59 pm India Standard Time on the day that falls six months after Launch. Clause 9 explains what happens if Launch is delayed, if the Game continues after Launch, and if the programme ends. Where we have a verified contact for you, we will try to remind you before your SO/ COINS expire. Not receiving a reminder does not change the expiry date.

Clauses 7.6 and 8 apply.

A7 International redemption

SO/ COINS can only be used on orders delivered in India. If we ever extend redemption to other countries, we will publish the countries, currency, tax and shipping treatment and which balances qualify before it starts.

Schedule C: Game Rules

C1 Cost. The Game is free to play.

C2 A game. Each game gives you three lives. A game ends when you lose your third life. Your Game Score is the total score shown at the end of that game.

C3 How many games. You can play as many games as you like. There is currently no daily limit. Only your Verified High Score counts towards SO/ COINS and the leaderboard.

C4 Controls. The controls are shown in the Game. You launch the ball by holding and releasing the launch control, and you play it using the flippers. You can pause the Game and change its settings from the game screen.

C5 How scores are made. SO/ COINS are scored when the ball hits the table's features, such as bumpers and targets. The table has no random bonus features. The ball's movement follows the Game's physics, so results depend on your skill and timing.

C6 Devices. The Game is designed for current versions of common desktop and mobile web browsers.

C7 Verification. Saved scores are checked on our servers before they become Verified High Scores, including a check that the score comes from a valid game session. A game that cannot be verified, for example because the connection was lost, may not count.

C8 Changes. We may change these Game Rules, the table and the scoring mechanics at any time under clause 14. Changes apply to games played after the change.
